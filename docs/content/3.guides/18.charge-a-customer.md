---
title: Charge a Customer
description: Take a one-time payment or start a plan, and send the customer a link to pay by card or mobile money.
---

There are two ways to charge someone: **once**, for an amount you choose, or **on a plan**, every billing period. Either way, the customer gets an invoice and a payment link, and the invoice is marked paid as soon as the payment is confirmed.

## In the dashboard

Use **Charge a customer** on the Overview or the Invoices page, or **Charge** in a customer's menu on the Customers page:

1. Choose the customer.
2. Choose **One-time amount** (what it's for, the amount and the currency) or **A plan** (the plan, a currency it's priced in, and optional trial days).
3. Choose how they pay:
   - **Send a payment link:** you get a link to copy into WhatsApp, SMS or email, or **Email it** to the customer from PimaBill.
   - **Already paid:** for cash, a bank transfer or a payment taken elsewhere. The invoice is recorded as paid, with the method.

## With the API

### Once

Create an invoice with `checkout` to get the payment link in the same call:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.pimabill.com/api/invoices \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "customerId": "cus_123",
    "currency": "UGX",
    "items": [{ "description": "Installation", "quantity": 1, "unitAmount": 150000 }],
    "dueDate": "2026-11-02",
    "checkout": { "callbackUrl": "https://shop.example.com/paid" }
  }'
```

The response is the invoice, plus `checkout.checkoutUrl` to send the customer. Pass `checkout.providerName` to use a specific provider; otherwise the highest-priority active one that can take the payment is used. The `invoice.paid` webhook tells you when it's paid.

Already paid? Create the invoice without `checkout`, then `POST /api/invoices/{id}/mark-paid` with `{ "paymentMethod": "cash" }`.

Selling a product from your catalogue, or taking a deposit and the rest later? See [One-time products & partial payments](/guides/one-time-products-and-partial-payments).

### On a plan

Subscribe the customer:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.pimabill.com/api/subscriptions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "customerId": "cus_123", "planId": "plan_home_20", "currency": "UGX" }'
```

The first invoice is created within a few seconds (unless there's a trial). Find it with `GET /api/invoices?subscriptionId={id}`, and get its payment link with `POST /api/invoices/{id}/checkout`. Renewals are invoiced and emailed automatically every period, and failed payments are retried. See [Subscriptions](/guides/subscriptions).

### Without an invoice

To take a payment that isn't a bill, such as a deposit or a top-up, use a [collection](/guides/money-movement#collect-a-payment): a mobile money PIN prompt or a hosted checkout, recorded in the ledger.

title: Ledger
description: Every payment, payout, fee and wallet movement as double-entry journal entries, checked for integrity.
---

Each organisation has a double-entry ledger. Settled payments, refunds, collections, payouts, transfers, wallet credits and platform commission are posted to it automatically as journal entries, so your finance team can reconcile every amount.

## Reading the ledger

| Endpoint | Returns |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/ledger/accounts` | The ledger's accounts and their balances per currency |
| `GET /api/ledger/entries` | Journal entries with their lines, newest first |
| `GET /api/ledger/health` | The latest integrity checks |
| `POST /api/ledger/sync` | Posts anything not yet in the ledger now (it also runs on its own) |

Every entry balances: its debits equal its credits in each currency. Entries are never edited; a correction is a new entry.

## Integrity checks

`GET /api/ledger/health` reports four checks:

| Check | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Debits equal credits in every currency | The ledger as a whole balances |
| Every journal entry balances | No single entry is lopsided |
| Account balances match their lines | Cached balances agree with the entries |
| Wallet balances match the ledger | Customer wallets agree with the ledger (informational) |

A failed check is shown in the dashboard's Ledger page, and the platform operator is alerted.

The ledger can be turned off for an organisation by its plan; see [Plan limits and access](/guides/plan-limits-and-access).

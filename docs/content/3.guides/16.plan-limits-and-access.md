---
title: Plan Limits and Access
description: What happens when your organisation reaches a plan limit, or its access is restricted.
---

On a hosted deployment, the platform operator can put your organisation on a plan with limits, and can restrict its access (for example while an invoice is overdue). The dashboard shows both, and the API returns clear errors your code can handle.

## Cloud plans

Every organisation on the cloud starts on **Free**, and can move to **Growth** at any time from **Plan** in the dashboard sidebar:

| | Free | Growth |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Free | About $10 a month in your currency, for example UGX 35,000, KES 1,250 or NGN 15,000; a year costs ten months |
| Customers | 1,000 | Unlimited |
| Active subscriptions | 1,000 | Unlimited |
| Invoices a month | 2,500 | Unlimited |
| Team members | 5 | Unlimited |
| Features, including managed payments | All | All |
| Support | Community | Priority |

Growth can be paid in every currency Flutterwave takes: UGX, KES, TZS, RWF, NGN, GHS, ZAR, XAF, XOF, ZMW, MWK, EGP, USD, EUR and GBP. Pick yours on the **Plan** page. Prices are set for African businesses first. The cloud earns mostly from the managed payments fee, so Free is generous, and Growth costs about as much as a monthly phone bundle.

The platform operator can also make an organisation a **Partner**. Partners are Cybroid's own products and its partners: they have no limits and pay nothing. The operator sets this in the control panel, under the organisation's **Plan and limits**.

Upgrading opens a checkout for your first invoice, payable by card or mobile money; the plan changes as soon as the payment is confirmed. Renewals are invoiced at the start of each period. If a renewal stays unpaid for 7 days after the paid period ends, the organisation returns to Free (nothing is deleted; only creating more than Free allows is refused). Cancel from the same page to return to Free at the end of the period you paid for.

Self-hosted deployments have no plans or limits.

## Plan limits

A plan can limit the number of customers, active subscriptions, invoices per month and team members, and can leave out features (usage billing, money movement, wallets, the ledger). When a request would go past a limit, it is refused with **402 Payment Required**:

```json
{
  "statusCode": 402,
  "code": "PLAN_LIMIT",
  "message": "Starter allows up to 100 customers.",
  "plan": "Starter",
  "upgradeUrl": "https://example.com/upgrade"
}
```

Reading is never limited: only creating more of a limited thing is refused. `upgradeUrl` is where to change plan, when the operator has set one. The dashboard shows your plan and its limits next to your organisation name.

## Restricted access

| Access | What works |
|---|---|
| Full | Everything |
| Read-only | Everything that reads; creating or changing anything is refused |
| Suspended | Nothing, except reading your organisation's details |

A refused request returns **403 Forbidden** with `code` `TENANT_READ_ONLY` or `TENANT_SUSPENDED`, the reason the operator gave as `message`, and `accessUrl` where to resolve it, if set. The dashboard shows the same reason in a banner.

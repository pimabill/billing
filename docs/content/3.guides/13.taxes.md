title: Taxes
description: Tax rates applied to invoices, by default or per customer, plan or charge.
---

## Create a tax rate

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.novabilling.one/api/taxes \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_a1b2c3d4..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "VAT",
    "code": "vat_18",
    "rate": 18,
    "description": "Uganda VAT",
    "appliedByDefault": true
  }'
```

`rate` is a percentage. A tax with `appliedByDefault: true` applies to every invoice unless something more specific says otherwise.

## Which taxes apply

The most specific assignment wins, checked in this order:

1. **Charge**: taxes on a usage charge (`POST /api/taxes/charge/{chargeId}`)
2. **Plan**: taxes on a plan (`POST /api/taxes/plan/{planId}`)
3. **Customer**: taxes for one customer (`POST /api/taxes/customer/{customerId}`), for example a different rate in another country
4. **Default**: every tax with `appliedByDefault`

Assign with `{ "taxId": "tax_123" }` in the body, list with `GET` on the same path, and remove with `DELETE .../{taxId}`. To exempt a customer, give them their own assignment (such as a 0% tax) so the defaults no longer apply.

Taxes are calculated after coupons and add-ons, and before prepaid wallet credit is used.

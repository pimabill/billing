---
title: Wallets
description: Prepaid credit that customers buy or you grant, used automatically on their invoices.
---

A wallet holds prepaid credit for one customer in one currency. When an invoice is created, credit in the customer's wallets is used first (after coupons, add-ons and taxes), and the invoice shows it as **Prepaid credits applied**. If the invoice is later voided, the credit goes back.

## Create a wallet

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.novabilling.one/api/wallets \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_a1b2c3d4..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "customerId": "cust_abc123",
    "name": "Main wallet",
    "currency": "UGX",
    "rateAmount": 1,
    "paidCredits": 50000,
    "grantedCredits": 5000
  }'
```

- **Paid credits** are credit the customer bought; **granted credits** are free credit you give (a welcome bonus or compensation). Both are spent the same way; they are recorded separately for your accounts.
- `rateAmount` is what one credit is worth in the wallet's currency (1 credit = 1 UGX above).
- `expirationAt` (ISO 8601) ends the wallet on that date; remaining credit is voided.

## Top up, grant or void

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.novabilling.one/api/wallets/transactions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_a1b2c3d4..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "walletId": "wal_123", "paidCredits": 20000 }'
```

Pass `grantedCredits` to give free credit, or `voidedCredits` to remove credit. `GET /api/wallets/{id}/transactions` lists every movement, and `DELETE /api/wallets/{id}` terminates a wallet and voids what is left.

## Moving money between wallets

[Transfers](/guides/money-movement#move-money-between-wallets) move value between two customers' wallets in the same currency, and a [payout](/guides/money-movement#send-a-payout) can be paid from a wallet with `walletId`.
